Who We Are

Loving God, Loving People, and Serving Our City.

At Kajiado Imarika Deliverence Church, we believe that faith is a journey best traveled together. No matter where you are in your spiritual walk, you are welcome here. We are a Christ-centered community committed to authentic relationships, biblical teaching, and meaningful outreach.
